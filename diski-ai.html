Privacy Policy for Diski AI

Effective date: 4 October 2026

Diski AI is developed and operated by DKane Sounds Pty Ltd. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Diski AI mobile application.

Information We Collect

Diski AI does not require users to create an account to access the app.

The app may process limited technical information necessary to provide its services, including device information, internet connection information, app version information, and diagnostic information related to errors or performance.

Diski AI connects to online services to retrieve football fixtures, predictions, competition information, and other football-related content.

Google Play Subscriptions

Diski AI offers a paid Premium subscription through Google Play.

Payments are processed by Google Play. Diski AI does not directly collect, store, or process your credit card, debit card, or other payment details.

Google may process payment and transaction information according to Google's own privacy policies. The Diski AI Premium subscription provides monthly access and is managed through the user's Google Play account.

Football Predictions

Diski AI provides AI-generated football predictions and analysis for informational and entertainment purposes.

Predictions are not guarantees of match results, betting outcomes, financial gain, or winnings. Users remain responsible for any decisions they make based on information displayed in the app.

Internet Access

Diski AI requires an internet connection to retrieve updated fixtures, predictions, subscription information, and other online content.

Third-Party Services

Diski AI may use third-party services necessary to operate the application, including Google Play Services, Google Play Billing, football data providers, and cloud-hosting services used to provide predictions and application data.

These services may process limited technical information according to their own privacy policies.

Notifications

If push notifications are enabled in Diski AI, users may receive notifications about new predictions, competition updates, app updates, or other Diski AI information. Users can control notification permissions through their device settings.

Data Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect information transmitted between the Diski AI application and its online services. However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

Children's Privacy

Diski AI is not designed specifically for children, and the service does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data Retention

Diski AI does not intentionally maintain personal user profiles within the application unless such functionality is introduced in a future version. Technical or operational data processed by third-party providers may be retained according to those providers' policies.

Your Choices

Users may uninstall Diski AI at any time, disable notifications through Android settings, manage or cancel their subscription through Google Play, and contact us regarding privacy-related questions.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Diski AI introduces new services, integrations, or features. Any updated version will include a revised effective date.
